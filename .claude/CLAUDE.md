# CLAUDE.md

## Language Settings

Documentation is written in English, but the developer is a Japanese speaker.
Always use Japanese for responses and code comments.

## Response Behavior

Interpret every message strictly according to its literal meaning.

For example, expressions such as "実装する" ("I will implement") or "実装したい" ("I want to implement") are nothing more than a sharing of intent. 
Only explicit directives such as "実装せよ" ("Implement it") or "実装すること" ("It must be implemented") may be interpreted as instructions to implement.
